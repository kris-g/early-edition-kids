Harry Kane wins the Ballon d'Or

England captain Harry Kane has won the Ballon d'Or. That is the golden ball trophy given to the best footballer in the world each year.

Kane was handed his prize at a big ceremony in London on 26 October. He is the first English player to win it for 25 years. The last one was Michael Owen in 2001.

Why did he win? Goals, goals, goals. Kane scored 61 times in 51 games for his club, Bayern Munich, last season & fans <loved> it.
