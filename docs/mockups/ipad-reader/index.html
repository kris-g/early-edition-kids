Early Edition on iPad

A working mock-up of the child's reader: the Kindle paper, with photos and colour. Every frame below is live, so you can tap through it here or open it full size. On an iPad, open reader.html and add it to the Home Screen.

  • Reading mode is a setting: ?mode=pages turns pages like the Kindle, ?mode=scroll runs the six stories down one long page. With a keyboard, M switches between them.
  • Type size follows the profile's age: ?age=6, 8 or 10.
  • The stories are sample text and the photos are stand-ins from Wikimedia Commons. Back issues and My week are not drawn yet.

Front page

The lead is the first unread story, and the other five are listed under it with a tick on each one read. This is the only place stories are chosen from.

A story, in pages

Nothing scrolls. Tap the right of the page, swipe, or use Next page. The running head leads back to the front page and shows which story of the six this is. On its side the iPad shows a spread, with the photo as the left-hand page.

A story with no photo

The topic's colour opens the story instead, so it never looks like something is missing.

End of a story

Turning on from the last page stamps the story as read and offers the next unread one.

A story, scrolling

The six stories run down one page. A story is stamped when its end comes into view, and the running head follows the story on screen.

By age

The same page for a younger and an older reader.
