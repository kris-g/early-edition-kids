Early Edition: the Sport page

Mock-ups for docs/sports-page.md. The matches, table and line-ups are the real round 11 of 21 to 23 November 2026, as the feeds in section 4 of the spec returned them, so the layouts are tested against real club and player names. The server fetches this once a day with the paper; the mock-up shows it as fetched on the morning of Monday 23 November. The data is in data.js.

Add ?frame=k-matches (or any id under a frame) to see one screen alone at full size.

On the Kindle

1072 × 1448, black and white, drawn at half size. Three tabs with no scrolling; the table is two pages of ten clubs so its type is big enough to read. The reader's club is Liverpool.

A match on the Kindle

Two screens: what happened, then the line-ups side by side. Nothing else is on them.

On the iPad

820 × 1180, portrait, in pages mode. The same three parts, with the paper's colours and the scorers under each result.

The way in

One strip on the front page under Today's paper. It never shows a score.
